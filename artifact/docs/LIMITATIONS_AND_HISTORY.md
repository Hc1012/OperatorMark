# Limits, negative results and original programme

The original goal was to study ownership of useful neural PDE operators under output, derivative and downstream-capability similarity. TangentMark was one candidate mechanism. Its failure under an attack is a result within that programme, not proof that the entire project failed.

## Findings that must remain visible

1. **Physical qualification was negative.** Q1–Q4 did not establish physical/continuum adequacy. The completed inverse task is a narrower discrete-reference, box-constrained task; the original physically qualified topology-dcap obligation remains unfulfilled/conditional.
2. **Initial marking failed.** The original 0/18 qualification result and failed/unavailable B1 remain. The study does not compare two working active watermarks.
3. **D3 was descriptive.** The corrected replay reproduced three saved endpoints in 960 updates. Noise classifications did not uniquely identify a cause, prove freedom from bugs, or qualify the original marks.
4. **P-N was completed.** A global-mean marking penalty produced nine candidates; all passed utility, only factor 3 qualified 3/3. The six failed qualification candidates remain negative evidence.
5. **Development detection loss was not qualified removal.** The later three marked development closures lost detection but failed derivative utility: 0/3 qualified removals.
6. **The main final test has an intact failure.** O05 lacks final intact detection. It remains in the ten-owner denominator and never counts as attack-induced removal.
7. **Ordinary attacks fail full utility.** Detection 0/10 after ordinary marked fine-tuning is not a successful removal result. The primary copy advantage and utility gate answer different questions.
8. **Successful A4/A5-B3 removal is bounded evidence.** It establishes neither minimal attack cost nor universal impossibility. Passive B2 remains positive at those endpoints; positivity is relative to fixed comparators, not a calibrated ownership probability.
9. **Student endpoints are budget-limited.** All seven marked terminal student cohorts fail full utility. This is not a convergence proof or a claim that all larger budgets must fail. Historical D1 still improved at its development cap; its one extension was exhausted.
10. **Equal healing journals are not fully explained.** Saved weights are distinct and show intended pruning observations, but the causal reason for equal loss journals remains unresolved. Later successful results-only merging does not rewrite the earlier archive-finalization failure.
11. **Null cases are dependent.** Zero of 210 fixed detections and zero of ten adaptive detections do not establish a population false-positive bound or universal resistance to false accusation.
12. **API and noise conclusions are narrow.** API results measure service responses only. The noise experiment uses a white-box pathwise convention, not stochastic black-box detection/removal.

## Separate earlier TPFA32 study

The earlier study contains ten owners, 10/10 qualified removals and 0/190 null detections. Its frozen statistical label is **trade-off/inconclusive**; copy-versus-scratch equivalence was not established. Its JVP geometric ratio is 1.1499 (97.5% interval [1.0919, 1.2109]); task ratio 0.9088 ([0.7087, 1.1654]). Its equivalence task interval falls below 0.8.

Those procedures, budgets, weak-owner/additive-slack gates and numerical setting differ from the present campaign. Do not combine their owners or nulls with the present N = 10 study. Saved historical summaries are included under `provenance/historical/`; no historical experiment was repeated for this release.

## Corpus and assurance amendments

D7's historical exclusion coverage is incomplete. D8 was adopted after generation and after 76 relaxed anomalies had been observed. Completed scientific generation, two readbacks and sealing remain completed evidence. They do not establish physical qualification, formal production acceptance or exclusive custody.

Formal S1/S2 historically passed for their then-tested bytes. S3_01 failed its setup cap. Later repairs cannot inherit that acceptance. E3, N0/N1 and host/provider/exclusive-custody/production assurance were explicitly deferred or left unsatisfied; a publication package does not satisfy them. Historical failed attempts and refusals remain private archival evidence, with their relevant scientific consequences reported here.

## Current scope disposition

All 130 intentionally deferred contract slots were subsequently bound. They are not missing experiments. The 50 clean-parent noise/API omissions and 400 unmeasured D2 task pairs are deliberate, separate decisions. Unmeasured is neither zero nor a negative measured result.

The retained original conditional obligations are buyer collusion, same-data ordinary controls, the 1,024-query ordinary tier, scratch-R4 solver attribution, training through wrappers, disjoint B0, black-box derivative protocols and N16. None is silently represented as completed. The numerical work selected under the adopted amendments is finished; the original unrestricted programme is not recast as wholly fulfilled.

Secondary results cover many correlated variants and endpoints. Only the fixed paired primary receives the stated inferential interpretation. Shared targets and a common development procedure limit independence and external validity. Recorded CPU scoring time is a scoped subtotal, not whole-project cost or equal-compute evidence.
